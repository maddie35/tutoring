What I help with

Every stage of the writing process

Most students were never actually taught how to plan and build an essay. That's not a personal failing; it's a missing lesson. Lessons can be taught.

Understanding the prompt

We break down what your instructor is actually asking for. No more writing a great essay for the wrong prompt.

Thesis statements

Turn a vague topic into a claim worth arguing. A good thesis holds the whole paper together.

Outlines

Plan the argument before you write. Drafting goes faster when you already know where you're headed.

Research papers

Find sources worth citing, use quotes well, and format them in MLA or APA without second-guessing every comma.

Revision

Spot the weak paragraph, the unclear sentence, the gap in your logic. Then fix all three.

Grammar and clarity

Fix the errors you keep repeating and build a voice that actually sounds like you.

How I teach

Learn in the way that works for you

Everyone's brain works differently. Sessions adapt to how you think, focus, and process, diagnosis or not.

This is a judgment-free space. Your identity, your pronouns, and the way your brain works are welcome here. So are essays about your own life.

  • Small, concrete stepsBig assignments become small tasks you can actually finish.
  • Visual planningColor-coded outlines and diagrams to see how your ideas connect.
  • Talk it out firstSay it out loud before you write it down. Talking beats staring at a blank page.
  • Flexible session lengthChoose shorter sessions or build in breaks.
  • Ideas before spellingEarly drafts are for thinking. Spelling and grammar come last, and never with judgment.
  • Work-along sessionsWrite while I'm there. Having someone in the room makes starting a lot easier.

FAQ

Common questions

Will you write my essay for me?

No. I help you become a stronger writer, and the work you turn in stays yours. That keeps you safe under your college's academic integrity policy, and it's the only way you actually get better.

What if I haven't started yet?

Perfect timing, actually. We can start from the prompt alone and build out from there.

Do I need a diagnosis to get this kind of support?

No. Sessions adapt to how you learn, and you never have to explain or prove anything.

Can we work around my accommodations?

Yes. You can bring them up whenever you're ready, or never. It's up to you.

Can I write about my identity or personal experiences?

Yes. Personal essays are welcome, and what you share in sessions stays between us.

Which classes do you help with?

English composition, plus any class that assigns essays: history, psychology, sociology, and the rest.

What if I can't afford tutoring?

Ask about the sliding scale. Sessions are $35 for students who need it, and you don't have to prove anything. The 15-minute intro call is free either way.

Can you help if my paper is due soon?

Often, yes. I take last-minute requests when I have space, so reach out as soon as you can. The earlier we start, the more we can build skills instead of just rescuing one paper.

Where do we meet?

In person at a public location near Diablo Valley College, such as a library or café, or online by video call.
