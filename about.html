About me

Hi, I'm Maddie. I help writing feel less like a chore.

I use any and all pronouns, I have strong opinions about outlines, and I want you to finish your next essay thinking, "Wait, that wasn't so bad."

Why I tutor

Writing is a skill, not a talent

A lot of students tell me they're "just bad at writing." Usually, nobody ever showed them how it works. Writing is a process with clear steps, and once you know the steps, the blank page gets a lot less scary.

I love writing, and I want you to stop dreading it. My goal isn't just a better grade on this paper. It's helping you build skills you'll use in every class, every job application, and every strongly worded email you'll ever send.

Why it matters to me

I've been there

I'm neurodivergent, so I know what it's like when your brain has 47 tabs open and none of them is the essay. I'm also queer, and I know how much it matters to learn somewhere you can show up as yourself.

That's why my sessions are flexible, judgment-free, and built around how you think. There's no single right way to write. There's just the way that works for you.
