# Writing tutoring site

A two-page site for essay and writing tutoring, hosted on GitHub Pages.

## Files

- `index.html`: Home page.
- `about.html`: About page.
- `styles.css`: Shared styles for both pages.
- `script.js`: Readable-font toggle for both pages.
- `maddie.jpg`: Your photo.

## Customize

Replace these placeholders:

- `[College Name]` in `index.html` and `about.html`
- `$[XX]` prices and `[Number]` of sessions
- `you@example.com` in the **Email a question** link in `index.html`
- `https://forms.gle/REPLACE-WITH-YOUR-FORM-LINK` in `index.html`, with your Google Form link

## Google Form questions

Include these questions on your request form:

- Name
- Email or phone number
- Class and assignment
- Due date
- Preferred days and times
- In person or online
- Pronouns (optional)
- Anything that helps you learn? (optional)

## Publish on GitHub Pages

1. Create a public repository, for example `writing-tutor`.
1. Upload all files to the repository root.
1. Go to **Settings** > **Pages**.
1. Under **Build and deployment**, set **Source** to **Deploy from a branch**.
1. Select the `main` branch and the `/ (root)` folder, then click **Save**.
1. After a minute or two, your site is live at `https://USERNAME.github.io/writing-tutor/`.

## Optional

- **Custom domain:** In **Settings** > **Pages**, enter a domain you own under **Custom domain**.
- **QR code:** Generate a QR code for your site URL and put it on flyers for campus bulletin boards.
- **Outreach:** Ask the college's Disabled Students Programs and Services (DSPS) office and its LGBTQ+ student club or pride center whether you can share flyers.
