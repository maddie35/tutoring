My teaching phiosophy

My teaching is rooted in learning science, Universal Design for Learning (UDL), and the belief that everyone deserves support that meets them where they're at.

How I tutor

Writing is a skill, not a talent

A lot of students tell me they're "just bad at writing." Usually, nobody ever showed them how it works. Writing is a process with clear steps, and once you know the steps, the blank page gets a lot less scary.

I love writing, and I want you to stop dreading it. My goal isn't just a better grade on this paper. It's helping you build skills you'll use in every class, every job application, and every strongly worded email you'll ever send.
