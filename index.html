How I teach

Learn in the way that works for you

Everyone's brain works differently. Sessions adapt to how you think, focus, and process information, whether or not you have a diagnosis.

This is a judgment-free space. Your identity, your pronouns, and the way your brain works are welcome here, and so are essays about your own experiences.

  • Small, concrete stepsBig assignments get broken into tasks you can finish one at a time.
  • Visual planningColor-coded outlines and diagrams to see how your ideas connect.
  • Talk it out firstSay your ideas out loud before writing them down. Talking is often faster than staring at a blank page.
  • Flexible session lengthChoose shorter sessions or build in breaks.
  • Ideas before spellingEarly drafts are for thinking. We fix spelling and grammar at the end, never with judgment.
  • Work-along sessionsWrite while I'm there with you. Having someone present can make it easier to start and stay focused.

FAQ

Common questions

Will you write my essay for me?

No. I help you become a stronger writer, and the work you submit stays yours. That protects you under your college's academic integrity policy, and it's how you actually improve.

What if I haven't started yet?

That's a great time to come in. We can work from the prompt alone, starting with brainstorming and an outline.

Do I need a diagnosis to get this kind of support?

No. Every session adapts to how you learn, and you never need to explain or prove anything.

Can we work around my accommodations?

Yes. You can bring them up whenever you're ready, or never. It's up to you.

Can I write about my identity or personal experiences?

Yes. Personal essays are welcome, and what you share in sessions stays between us.

Which classes do you help with?

English composition and any class that assigns essays or papers, such as history, psychology, and sociology.

What if I can't afford tutoring?

Ask about the sliding scale. Sessions are $35 for students who need it, and you don't have to prove anything. You can also start with a free 15-minute intro call.

Can you help if my paper is due soon?

Often, yes. I take last-minute requests when I have space, so reach out as soon as you can. Starting earlier gives us more time to build your skills, not just finish one paper.

Where do we meet?

In person at a public location near [College Name], such as a library or café, or online by video call.
